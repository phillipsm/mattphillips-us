# Illustrations

<p class="lede">Simple drawings can instantly explain a complex topic. They give energy and personality to interfaces. They enhance text. They're core.</p>

<img src="/assets/illustrations/ill-hero@2x.png" alt="Illustration — wall-mounted light fixture" width="2200" height="2200" />

## Process

Most of my graphics start as pencil-and-paper sketches. Then I photograph the sketch, place it in Adobe Illustrator, and work on stroke weight, balance, and color.
