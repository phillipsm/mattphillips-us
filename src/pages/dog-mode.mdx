## The product

Dog Mode uses Google Home's open APIs to automatically adjust your lights, thermostat, TV, and sound devices — creating the perfect environment for your dog whenever you're away.

The app connects to Matter-compatible smart home devices through Google Home. With one tap, it creates a custom environment tailored to your dog:

- **Climate.** Ideal temperature based on your dog's breed and size, or off entirely.
- **Light.** Optimized levels and tones designed for dogs.
- **Sight.** Dynamic nature content that keeps your dog mentally stimulated.
- **Sound.** Music and natural soundscapes on a comforting daily rhythm.

## Building with AI

Almost as important as the app itself is *how* I built it. Dog Mode is the first product I've taken end-to-end with AI coding tools in the driver's seat — Claude Code as the primary engineer, with me as the designer, reviewer, and editor.

Along the way I built the [Styling Drift MCP](/styling-drift-mcp) — a tool that catches design-system drift as the model writes code, so consistency is enforced in the loop rather than patched up later.
